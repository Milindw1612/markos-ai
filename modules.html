Full Specification

14 modules, spec'd end to end

Everything a Digital Marketing Director's command centre needs to cover — from campaign planning to marketing mix modelling. Four modules (marked flagship) have a working interactive demo; the rest are specified here at design depth.

01

Campaign Intelligence Engine

  • Campaign planning wizard with AI brief generation from a one-line goal
  • Visual campaign calendar with conflict detection and seasonal intelligence
  • Channel Fit Scorer — AI recommends the optimal channel mix per campaign, product, and audience
  • A/B test framework with auto-pause on losing variants
  • AI-generated media plan on request: reach, frequency, GRPs, and weekly budget phasing, exportable as a PDF for the CEO or agency partners
02

Performance Marketing Hub

  • Unified KPI dashboard: ROAS, ROI, CAC, LTV, MQLs, SQLs in one view
  • Multi-touch attribution (Last Click, Linear, Data-Driven, MTA)
  • Bid strategy advisor with an automated bid-rules engine, plus dayparting control per platform
  • Creative fatigue detection — auto-flags when ad frequency crosses 3.5–5x on Meta / 4–6x on LinkedIn and queues a replacement from the Content Studio
  • Audience saturation monitor — recommends lookalike expansion once reach passes 60–70% of the defined audience
  • Landing page quality-score tracker with automatic A/B test suggestions when CVR drops
03

Budget & Finance Control

  • Annual budget builder with channel-level allocation
  • Real-time budget pacing with overspend/underspend alerts
  • One-click budget reallocation between campaigns
  • Automated pacing rules — e.g. if a channel overspends 10% by day 15, excess is redistributed to the highest-ROAS alternative automatically
  • Dynamic pricing intelligence — for D2C/e-commerce lines, surfaces competitor pricing and recommends promotional discount depth and timing based on conversion-elasticity modelling, so offers are set without leaving margin on the table
04

Built-in CRM + CDP

  • Full CRM: contacts, pipeline, lead scoring, lifecycle stages
  • Email + WhatsApp automation with a drip sequence builder
  • Intent data overlay — B2B intent signals surfaced directly on CRM contacts, so in-market accounts get flagged to Sales before they've raised a hand
  • Review management — Google/Trustpilot/marketplace reviews pulled in with a sentiment score per product line and AI-drafted responses for negative reviews
  • HubSpot / Salesforce bidirectional sync — for teams already invested in one of these, so Sales and Marketing keep working off the same lead record instead of two disagreeing systems (see the Tool Stack)
  • Competitor-triggered nurture — when a known contact starts researching a competitor, MarkOS auto-fires a comparison-focused sequence and flags the account "at risk" to Sales
05

AI Content Studio

  • AI copywriter for ads, email, landing pages, blogs, and social captions with brand-voice training
  • Dynamic landing page builder with real-time personalisation by UTM, device, and known lead score — a B2B CFO and an SME founder can see different copy from the same ad
  • Image/video generation stack for brand assets, product photography, and short-form video, plus multilingual AI voiceover
  • AI presentation builder — a brief in, a polished CEO update deck or campaign-results presentation out, exportable as PPTX/PDF
  • Podcast/audio ad creator: script → AI voice → scheduled distribution, with the transcript auto-repurposed into blog and social content
  • Creative fatigue → auto content request — a declining creative doesn't just get flagged, it opens a pre-filled brief with AI-generated replacement variants ready for approval
06

Social Automation Layer

  • Multi-platform publishing: LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky, Snapchat
  • n8n workflow export for every social campaign — this is the one module with a real, running automation on this site (see the Reporting demo and the automation setup guide)
  • Natively replaces Hootsuite, Buffer, and Predis.ai — visual, copy, hashtags, and scheduling generated and queued in one flow, not three separate tools
  • LinkedIn automation and employee-advocacy module
  • Reels/Shorts script-to-publish pipeline
  • Social listening layer — daily brand and competitor sentiment score surfaced directly on the Director's morning brief
  • Push notification & in-app marketing — for companies with a mobile app, AI-personalised push content, per-user send-time optimisation, deep-link targeting, and revenue attribution alongside the email/WhatsApp channels
07

Team & Resource Management

  • Org chart, role management, and RBAC permissions
  • Campaign-linked task boards, AI task generator, and a workload view
  • OKR tracking wired to live CRM and campaign data — not a static spreadsheet
  • Agency and freelancer management with invoice tracking
  • AI weekly debrief — every Monday, each team member gets a personalised summary of what mattered in their lane; the Director gets the cross-team roll-up. No status meeting required
08

AI Recommendation Engine Flagship — live demo

  • Live campaign alerts: SCALE, PAUSE, SHIFT, BID, ALERT — one-click approve/snooze/dismiss with a confidence score and estimated impact
  • Full audit trail of every AI suggestion and what happened after it was actioned
  • Competitive response alerts — a competitor's major launch or price drop is detected via ad-library monitoring and a counter-strategy is surfaced within the hour
  • Funnel leak detector — when any funnel stage conversion drops >15% week-over-week, the AI diagnoses whether it's a demand, lead-quality, or sales-response problem and proposes a specific fix

Channel Intelligence & Audience Mapping

  • Channel Fit Matrix — product × audience × goal → a ranked channel recommendation
  • Lookalike seed-audience builder for Meta, Google, and LinkedIn
  • Audience overlap detector across concurrent campaigns, to catch internal cannibalisation before it wastes spend

Competitive Intelligence Engine Flagship — live demo

  • Daily competitor ad-creative tracking across Meta, Google, and LinkedIn ad libraries
  • Estimated competitor media spend by channel and Share of Voice (SOV) across paid and organic
  • Weekly AI-written competitive brief: what launched, what's working, and a recommended response
  • Competitive traffic intelligence — estimated traffic, sources, and audience overlap for any competitor's site

11

Reporting & Dashboard Engine Flagship — live demo

  • Director's Command Centre — Revenue, Blended ROAS, Spend, MQLs, Pipeline Value, CAC, LTV, and Budget Remaining on one screen with green/amber/red status vs. target
  • On-demand reports in plain English — "Show me ROAS by channel for July" generates the chart and narrative instantly; exportable as PDF, PPTX, Excel, or an embeddable link. Powered by Claude for the narrative and deck generation and Julius AI for ad-hoc statistical questions outside the pre-built dashboards — see the Tool Stack
  • Scheduled reports — daily morning brief, Monday weekly report, and an auto-generated monthly board deck, delivered by email, Slack, WhatsApp, or in-app
  • Multi-channel data aggregation into one normalised layer, refreshed every 4 hours, with history retained back to account inception (not just the last 90 days)
  • AI forecasting: 30/60/90-day and 12-month revenue forecasts, budget-to-revenue "what if" simulation, and cohort LTV forecasting
  • This is the module with a real working automation on this site — a daily n8n-generated morning brief. See it below.

12

ROI / ROAS Intelligence & Marketing Mix Modeling Flagship — live demo

  • True ROI engine — nets out agency fees, content production cost, tool cost, and team time against ad spend, and factors in gross margin so ROI reflects actual profit, not just revenue
  • Marketing Mix Modelling — a statistical layer (in the spirit of Google Meridian / Meta Robyn) estimating the true incremental contribution of each channel, controlling for seasonality and competitor activity — the thing last-click attribution can't answer
  • Saturation curves per channel, informing an explicit "you're overspending on X, underspending on Y" allocation call
  • Incrementality testing — geo/audience holdouts and synthetic controls that can override last-click ROAS when they disagree
  • Budget scenario simulator — target a revenue number, get the optimal channel allocation to hit it; run "what if CPMs rise 20%" style scenarios instantly
Try the budget scenario simulator →
13

Sales Funnel Analytics

  • Tracks every stage from Awareness → Engagement → Lead → MQL → SAL/SQL → Opportunity → Revenue, with volume, conversion rate, and cost tracked at every stage
  • Auto-calculated KPIs: Lead→MQL rate, MQL→SQL rate, win rate, funnel velocity (days to close), cost per MQL, pipeline coverage ratio, and a single 0–100 Funnel Health Score
  • MQL Velocity Watchdog — if Sales doesn't contact an MQL within SLA, MarkOS auto-nurtures the lead, escalates to the Sales Manager, and logs the breach — so a slow funnel gets correctly blamed on response time, not lead quality
  • Closed-Won feedback loop — every won deal's profile feeds back into lead scoring and lookalike targeting automatically; every lost deal (with reason) suppresses that persona from future high-spend channels
  • Revenue attribution waterfall — a live campaign-level P&L (spend vs. attributed revenue), not just ROAS, so the Director can see which campaigns are actually in the black
14

Live Campaign Dashboard

  • Per-campaign health indicator (🟢/🟡/🔴), spend-pacing bar, live ROAS gauge, and a running MQL counter — refreshed every 2–4 hours
  • Creative performance panel: top/bottom performing creatives ranked by CTR, conversion, and fatigue score, with one-click pause or refresh
  • Four display modes: Director Mode (all campaigns, one screen), Campaign Deep-Dive (full breakdown), War Room / TV Mode (rotating office-screen display), and a Mobile Morning Brief widget
Signature Automations

What no single MarTech tool does today

All 10 automations from the full spec that were the actual motivation for designing MarkOS AI in the first place — none of these exist as a native feature in any one platform.

🔁 MQL Velocity Watchdog
If Sales doesn't contact an MQL within SLA, auto-nurtures the lead, escalates to the Sales Manager, and flags the source campaign for follow-up failure — so slow response isn't blamed on lead quality.
🧠 Closed-Won Feedback Loop
Every won deal's profile feeds back into lead scoring and lookalike audiences automatically; every lost deal (with reason) suppresses that persona from future high-spend channels.
🚨 Funnel Leak Detector
Diagnoses why a conversion rate dropped — demand, lead quality, or sales response — and drafts the fix, not just the alert.
📊 Pipeline Coverage Auto-Alert
Checks pipeline coverage every Monday; if it drops below 3x, drafts the recommended campaign brief to close the gap before the quarter is at risk.
🕵️ Competitor-Triggered Nurture
When intent data shows an existing lead researching a competitor, auto-fires a comparison case study and flags a "Competitive Risk" on their CRM record — recovers at-risk deals before formal evaluation.
🎨 Creative Fatigue → Auto Content Request
A declining creative doesn't just get flagged — it opens a pre-filled Content Studio brief with AI-generated replacement variants ready for a 10-minute approval, not a 2-hour production cycle.
💰 Revenue Attribution Waterfall
A live campaign P&L — spend vs. attributed revenue — so budget decisions are made on profit contribution, not ROAS alone.
📅 Auto-Scheduled Board Report
Compiles the full monthly board deck automatically, delivered by 6 AM the Friday it's due — the same pattern this site's live n8n automation demonstrates at daily scale.
🌐 Dynamic Landing Page Personalisation
Reads UTM, device, and known lead score to reassemble the headline, hero, social proof, and CTA per visitor in real time — a B2B CFO and an SME founder see different pages from the same ad.
🤖 AI Weekly Campaign Debrief
Every Monday, each team member gets a personalised summary of only their campaigns and tasks; the Director gets the cross-team roll-up. No status meeting required.
Director's Wish List — 2026 Market Gaps

Additional capabilities on the roadmap

Gaps identified from a review of the current AI marketing tool landscape — high-value features leading platforms are adding, or that remain underserved. Not yet modelled in depth; flagged here as scope for a v3 revision.

Partner & Affiliate Marketing Management
An Impact.com/PartnerStack-style module: recruit affiliates, track referral revenue, manage commissions, and report affiliate ROAS alongside paid media — critical where affiliates/creators drive 15–30% of D2C revenue.
Influencer CRM & Campaign Management
Search creators by niche, audience, fake-follower score, and brand-safety history; brief, track deliverables, measure earned media value, and log payments without leaving the platform.
Voice of Customer (VoC) Intelligence
Aggregates NPS, reviews, support tickets, and social sentiment into one feed; AI surfaces recurring themes — e.g. "customers are comparing you to Competitor X on price" — straight into messaging decisions.
WhatsApp Business Marketing Suite
Full WhatsApp Business API module — broadcast campaigns, chatbot flows, catalogue sharing, abandoned cart recovery — given WhatsApp's 80%+ open rates vs. ~20% for email across India, MENA, and SE Asia.
AI-Powered Media Planning
Generates a full media plan from a campaign brief — reach, frequency, GRPs, weekly budget phasing — exportable as a PDF for the CEO or agency partners, before a single rupee is spent.
Google SGE & AI Search Optimisation
Goes beyond keyword rank tracking: AI Overview appearance tracking, conversational query analysis, and content structured to be cited by AI search engines, not just ranked in blue links.
Programmatic Advertising Management
DV360 and The Trade Desk integration for display, video, and CTV — set up, monitor, and optimise programmatic campaigns without switching to a separate DSP interface.
Customer Journey Orchestration Map
A visual map of actual 3-, 5-, and 7-step conversion paths from CRM and attribution data, with AI recommending which touchpoints to invest in to shorten the journey and lift conversion.
