Integration & Replacement Map

The full tool stack, by function

A Digital Marketing Director's actual daily stack runs to 15–25 point solutions. This is the complete list MarkOS AI is specified to either natively replace or integrate with — organised by the function it serves, with a one-line "Daily use" for each: not what the tool does in general, but the specific moment in the Director's day or week it earns its place.

Powers the Conversational Layer

AI Assistant & Analytics

Conversational AI Assistant
Claude (Anthropic)
Powers the plain-English query interface ("How much did we spend on Meta last month?"), drafts board decks and reports as real files via Agent Skills, and is the actual LLM running this site's own n8n morning-brief automation.
Daily use: the 7 AM brief and the Friday board deck are both, functionally, a Claude output.
Autonomous Knowledge-Work Agent
Claude Cowork
Delegates multi-step, multi-app tasks — compiling the board report from Drive, Gmail, and Slack, chasing blockers, drafting the weekly debrief — end to end, with citations back to the source files.
Daily use: hand it "compile Friday's board deck" on Monday; it works the whole week and hands back a cited draft, not a to-do list.
AI Data Analyst
Julius AI
Chat-with-your-data for the question no pre-built dashboard answers — connects to Snowflake/BigQuery/Postgres and runs statistical analysis without a data analyst on call.
Daily use: the 2-minute follow-up question right after the morning brief flags a number that looks off.
Module 11

Reporting & Data Aggregation

Data Aggregation — pick by scale
Supermetrics / Funnel.io / Improvado / Adverity
Four vendors, one job: normalise 100+ marketing platforms into one layer. MarkOS's own Measure & Optimise agent fetches its 5 core paid channels (Meta, Google, LinkedIn, Pinterest) directly via each platform's native API — cheaper and simpler to maintain at that scale, so this tier is reserved for platforms beyond that core set, or once channel/client count grows large enough that in-house integration upkeep costs more than licensing. Supermetrics for mid-market cross-platform reporting; Funnel.io and Improvado for enterprise multi-source warehousing; Adverity for complex multi-market pipelines.
Daily use: the fallback once you're onboarding a channel outside the core 5, or running this for several client brands at once — not needed for MarkOS's own reconciled reporting today.
Automated Reporting — pick by client model
Whatagraph / AgencyAnalytics
White-labelled, automated stakeholder reports. Whatagraph for direct client/stakeholder reporting; AgencyAnalytics once you're managing several client brands under one roof.
Daily use: the monthly report that used to take 4–6 hours, now scheduled and delivered on its own.
Live Dashboard
Databox
Always-on KPI scorecards, TV/war-room display ready for the marketing command centre.
Daily use: the always-on screen in the marketing war room during a launch week.
BI & Visualisation
Looker Studio (Google)
Free BI layer with AI narrative summaries — exports MarkOS reports for non-login stakeholder viewing.
Daily use: sharing a live number with a stakeholder who doesn't have, and shouldn't need, a MarkOS login.
SEO Reporting
SEMrush / Ahrefs
Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlink growth, and content gap analysis.
Daily use: the weekly SEO check-in with the content team.
Module 12

Budget, ROI & Marketing Mix Modelling

MMM — used together, not either/or
Google Meridian / Meta Robyn
Two open-source MMM engines run alongside each other, not as substitutes — Meridian as the primary model, Robyn (especially strong for Meta-heavy advertisers) to cross-validate the same channel-attribution answer before it goes to the CFO.
Daily use: the quarterly budget-reallocation call, backed by a model instead of a hunch — and a second model to defend it.
Incrementality Testing
Measured
Geo and audience holdout experiments proving which spend genuinely drives new revenue vs. what would've happened anyway.
Daily use: settling the "is this channel actually working" argument with a holdout test, not an opinion.
Bid & Budget Automation
Skai / Optmyzr
Skai handles omnichannel bid optimisation across Google, Meta, Amazon, and retail media; Optmyzr layers no-code, AI-suggested automated rules on top. Start with Skai alone for a simpler stack, add Optmyzr when you need granular rule-based control.
Daily use: the single lever pulled when reallocating budget mid-week; the automated rule that pauses an underperforming ad group at 2 AM.
D2C Attribution — pick by vendor
Rockerbox / Triple Whale / Northbeam
Three vendors for the same job — first-party, iOS-resistant D2C attribution. Rockerbox pairs first-party pixel data with MTA and MMM; Triple Whale and Northbeam focus on real-time blended ROAS, MER, and true CAC.
Daily use: the D2C attribution source of truth when Meta's and Google's own numbers disagree.
Module 05

AI Content & Creative

AI Image — used together, not either/or
Recraft / Ideogram / Flux Pro
Three specialists, not substitutes: Recraft for vector brand assets with brand-kit enforcement (replaces Canva AI); Ideogram for text-accurate imagery — product names or slogans rendered correctly inside the visual; Flux Pro for photorealistic product/lifestyle photography without a shoot.
Daily use: the brand-compliant asset produced in minutes instead of a 3-day turnaround; a product name spelled correctly in the image, first try; product photography for a new SKU, no photoshoot booked.
AI Video — used together, not either/or
Kling / Sora / Pika
Kling for everyday text-to-video and Reels; Sora for the higher-fidelity luxury brand film where visual quality can't be a compromise; Pika isn't a generator at all — it's the editing layer that polishes whatever Kling or Sora produce.
Daily use: a product demo briefed this afternoon; the hero brand film for a luxury launch; polishing generated footage into something publish-ready.
Video Repurposing
Opus Clip
Long video in, 5–10 short-form Reels/Shorts out, with auto-captions and hooks scored by predicted engagement.
Daily use: turning one long-form video into a week of Reels before lunch.
AI Presentations
Gamma.app
Brief in, polished CEO update deck or campaign-results presentation out, in seconds — the platform's primary deck-creation tool.
Daily use: the board deck, built from a brief in the time it takes to make coffee.
AI Copywriting
Jasper / Copy.ai
Ad, email, and landing-page copy with brand-voice training built natively into the Content Studio.
Daily use: the ad copy variant test running before the team's first standup.
AI Voice
ElevenLabs
Hyper-realistic voiceover in 30+ languages, with brand-ambassador voice cloning.
Daily use: a voiceover in a regional language without booking a studio.
Ad Creative at Scale — pick by vendor
AdCreative.ai / Smartly.io
Same job: hundreds of creative variants generated and scored by predicted conversion. AdCreative.ai as the standalone generator; Smartly.io if you want that automation wired directly into live ad-platform performance data.
Daily use: 20 creative variants ranked by predicted performance before a campaign even launches; the automatic refresh the moment fatigue is detected.
