Integration & Replacement Map

The full tool stack, by function

A Digital Marketing Director's actual daily stack runs to 15–25 point solutions. This is the complete list MarkOS AI is specified to either natively replace or integrate with — organised by the function it serves, with a one-line "Daily use" for each: not what the tool does in general, but the specific moment in the Director's day or week it earns its place.

Powers the Conversational Layer

AI Assistant & Analytics

Conversational AI Assistant
Claude (Anthropic)
Powers the plain-English query interface ("How much did we spend on Meta last month?"), drafts board decks and reports as real files via Agent Skills, and is the actual LLM running this site's own n8n morning-brief automation.
Daily use: the 7 AM brief and the Friday board deck are both, functionally, a Claude output.
Autonomous Knowledge-Work Agent
Claude Cowork
Delegates multi-step, multi-app tasks — compiling the board report from Drive, Gmail, and Slack, chasing blockers, drafting the weekly debrief — end to end, with citations back to the source files.
Daily use: hand it "compile Friday's board deck" on Monday; it works the whole week and hands back a cited draft, not a to-do list.
AI Data Analyst
Julius AI
Chat-with-your-data for the question no pre-built dashboard answers — connects to Snowflake/BigQuery/Postgres and runs statistical analysis without a data analyst on call.
Daily use: the 2-minute follow-up question right after the morning brief flags a number that looks off.

Module 12

Budget, ROI & Marketing Mix Modelling

MMM — Open Source
Google Meridian
Quantifies true incremental revenue per channel, controlling for seasonality and external factors — replaces last-click guesswork for annual planning.
Daily use: the quarterly budget-reallocation call, backed by a model instead of a hunch.
MMM — Alternative Engine
Meta Robyn
Meta's open-source MMM, particularly strong for Meta-heavy advertisers — run alongside Meridian to cross-validate.
Daily use: cross-checking Meridian's recommendation before defending it to the CFO.
Incrementality Testing
Measured
Geo and audience holdout experiments proving which spend genuinely drives new revenue vs. what would've happened anyway.
Daily use: settling the "is this channel actually working" argument with a holdout test, not an opinion.
Omnichannel Bid Mgmt
Skai (Kenshoo)
Cross-platform bid optimisation across Google, Meta, Amazon, and retail media simultaneously.
Daily use: the single lever pulled when reallocating budget mid-week across platforms.
PPC Optimisation
Optmyzr
Automated bid/budget/audience rules with AI-suggested templates — no-code power-user automation.
Daily use: the automated rule that pauses an underperforming ad group at 2 AM so no one has to.
Attribution + MMM
Rockerbox
First-party pixel data combined with MTA and MMM — strong for D2C and e-commerce brands.
Daily use: the D2C attribution source of truth when Meta's and Google's own numbers disagree.
E-commerce ROAS
Triple Whale / Northbeam
Pixel-level tracking that survives iOS restrictions; blended ROAS, MER, and true CAC in real time.
Daily use: the real ROAS number, post-iOS-tracking-changes, that the Director actually trusts.
Enterprise Analytics
Adverity
Automated data aggregation and ROI analysis for complex, multi-market enterprise pipelines.
Daily use: the multi-market ROI rollup ahead of a global marketing review.
