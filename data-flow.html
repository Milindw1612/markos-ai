Putting It All Together

How the five pieces connect

Same data as the five sections below, wired into a single loop — with the actual tools and the module number each stage maps back to, the channels posts actually publish to, and who at each org level owns that stage of the work.

Read left to right, top to bottom, with the org spine on the left showing who owns each stage: the Digital Marketing Team (micro level) runs Plan & Create (Modules 01, 05, 06, 09) off a Director brief and publishes to YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and Google Ads — once those posts go live, the resulting spend and performance data is exactly what feeds Measure & Optimise (Modules 02, 03, 08, 12), alongside the separately Gateway-fed Convert & Retain (Modules 04, 10, 13). All three report into Report & Lead (Modules 07, 11, 14) — closing out all 14 modules between the four clusters — owned by the Digital Marketing Manager (macro level 1), which trades computed metrics for narrative with the cross-cutting AI Assistant Layer (no single module owns it) before the combined dashboard reaches the Head / Director (macro level 2) — who takes it from there into the CMO → CEO → Board cascade on the Helicopter View.
