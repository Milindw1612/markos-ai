Read Before the Diagram

The 8-layer architecture, in plain language

LayerWhat lives hereRepresentative tools
Data Sources Ad platforms, CRM, e-commerce, web analytics, and social listening — the raw signal MarkOS ingests every few hours, before any module touches it. Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, HubSpot, Shopify, GA4, Brandwatch
Aggregation Gateway Normalises every source into one warehouse first, so every downstream number — a KPI tile, a chart, an AI recommendation — traces back to the same ground truth. Supermetrics, Funnel.io, Improvado
Plan & Create Where campaigns, content, and channel strategy get built. Modules 01, 05, 06, 09. Recraft, Ideogram, Kling, Sora, Gamma.app, Hootsuite/Buffer
Measure & Optimise Where spend turns into a verdict — and a recommendation. Modules 02, 03, 08, 12. Skai, Optmyzr, Google Meridian, Meta Robyn, Albert.ai
Convert & Retain Where leads become pipeline and pipeline becomes revenue. Modules 04, 10, 13. HubSpot/Salesforce, MadKudu, Clari, Sprinklr
Report & Lead Where the Director actually sees the business, and the team gets managed. Modules 07, 11, 14. Databox, Whatagraph, Notion AI/Monday.com
AI Assistant Layer The conversational and delegation layer sitting across reporting and recommendations, not owned by any single module. Claude, Claude Cowork, Julius AI
Director / Feedback Loop Every approval writes back down into live campaigns, budget, and CRM — the system doesn't just report, it closes the loop. Human-in-the-loop, every action
